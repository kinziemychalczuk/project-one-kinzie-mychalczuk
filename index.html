Why Rebus Puzzles Just Make Sense

I always loved looking at a strange composition of words and trying to find out what it means. How does one make that connection? How did I know that "M1Y L1I1F1E" means "for once in my life"? Is there a possible explanation as to how they are figured out or made in the first place?

Connecting The Pieces

Solving a pictogram, or a phrase made of images and glyphs, tends to be a subconcious task. The same way that Emojis envoke specific emotions and words, the replacement of words with images conveys the message just as well. An image of an eye is easily processed as "I", the letter C can be used in place of "see", U instead of "you", and so on.

Eye Spy a Little Eye

Other kinds of Rebus puzzles involve the format, typography, and positioning of the words. The weight of the font, stacking the words, the amount of times the words appear; these are different kinds of the puzzles.
