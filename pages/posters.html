How Posters Advertise Movies So Well

What makes a movie poster so iconic? Why does a long-awaited sequel recreate the original movie's poster? How does it make someone want to pay to go see it? Does the layout, composition, and/or positive/negative space have anything to do with it?

The Best Posters of All Time

The most iconic posters did the best job at showing what the movie was about, and what it would feel like. Every scary movie was clearly shown as scary in the poster, but not scary enough that some kid going to see another movie would have nightmares.

The Worst Posters of All Time

Many modern movie posters have very common issues. They may be Photoshopped to the point that nobody looks as they should, it looks like an AI-generated pretzel of body parts, cheesy poses, way too much, way too little, etc.
